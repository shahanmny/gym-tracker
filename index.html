Deload week. Do 2 sets instead of 3 on everything, same weights. One week only, then back to normal. This is how you keep progressing, not a week off.

The one rule. The weight does not go up until you have hit the top of the rep range on every set. Not when you feel ready, not when a schedule says so, only when the log proves it.

That's the whole difference between this and the last program. You walk out of the chamber having earned every rung you climbed.

What to do next session

Log your sets above and each exercise tells you its own next target. The rule behind it:

Last session you…Next session
Hit the top of the range on all setsNext dumbbell setting, drop back to the bottom of the range
Improved on last sessionSame weight, add +1 rep to as many sets as you can
No improvement at the same weightRun it back. If it happens twice, drop one setting

Reps first, weight second. Weight only moves when the reps have nowhere left to go.

Landing below the rep range after a jump is normal. Your dumbbells step in 2.5 lb increments below 25 lb and 5 lb above, at light weights that's a 33% or even 50% jump, so of course the reps fall off a cliff. That is the system working, not you failing.

A real stall is different: the same weight two sessions running with no improvement in total reps. That's the only thing this page treats as a problem, and even then the fix is one setting down, not ten percent.

Increment size: whatever the next real setting is. The SelectTech 552 does 2.5 lb steps from 5 to 25 lb, then 5 lb steps from 25 to 50, with a final rung at 52.5. Every target here snaps to a real setting, it will never ask you for a weight the dial can't make.

The wall is at 25 lb. Below it the steps are gentle (a 2.5 lb jump at 20 lb is +13%). The 25 → 30 jump is +20%, and it's the first one that will genuinely stop you. Expect two or three exposures before a new weight clicks up there, and bridge it with mixed sets rather than grinding: take the new weight on the sets you can hold, drop back for the rest.

Week 1 is calibration

The starting numbers come from what you actually logged in your old sheet, so they're real. But floor press → bench press is harder now that you're working the full range, and some of those old numbers were too light.

Treat every starting weight as a guess. Blow past the top of the range and jump 5 lb immediately. Can't reach the bottom, drop down. By week 2 you'll have honest numbers and the rule takes over.

Bands, not promises. ⌐ marks the lifts that will hit the 50 lb ceiling somewhere in Block C, backs and legs get strong fastest. That's expected, 52.5 lb is simply the end of the ladder.

When a lift caps out you stop adding weight and start adding difficulty: 3-second lowering, a 2-second pause at the bottom, 1.5-reps, or switch to single-limb. Those buy months more progress on the same dumbbells.

Rest and tempo

ItemPrescription
Effort, sets 1 & 2RIR 2–3
Effort, set 3RIR 1–2
Rest, exercises 1–22–3 min
Rest, exercises 3–560–90 sec
Lowering phase2 sec, controlled
Warm-up sets1–2 light, first exercise only

RIR means “reps in reserve”, how many more you could have done if you'd had to. RIR 2 means you stopped with two left in the tank. Sets 1 and 2 stop at RIR 2–3; the last set goes harder, to RIR 1–2. Without this, “my reps went up” might only mean you tried harder that day, and the whole progression rule stops meaning anything.

Warm-up sets don't count as working sets and don't get logged. Once arm work passes 30 lb, add an extra warm-up set and slow the lowering down further.

Calories run higher than standard because you run. If the scale hasn't moved in two weeks, add 250 calories and hold. Repeat until it moves. Every mile is roughly 100 calories you need to put back before you're even at maintenance.

Running

Keep it, it's good for you. Run on off days, or after lifting on lift days, never before. Just be clear with yourself that it's a cost against the mass goal, not a leg workout. That's what Day 3 is for.

One purchase worth making. A $25 doorway pull-up bar. It's the only real gap left in this program, you have no vertical pull. Once you have one, swap the DB Pullover on Day 2 for pull-ups, or slow negatives if you can't do one yet.
